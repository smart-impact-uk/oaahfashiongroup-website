Governance, Compliance & Disclosures

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC operates in full accordance with applicable corporate, consumer protection, financial, and data privacy regulations. The disclosures below summarize our standing policies and binding commitments.

01

Privacy Policy

+

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC ("the Company") is committed to safeguarding the personal information of its customers, vendors, corporate partners, and stakeholders. Our data handling practices are aligned with all applicable global data protection regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and any other state, federal, or international statutes governing the processing of personal information.

The Company adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization. We collect only the information strictly necessary for transactional processing, regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, and the operational continuity of the Company's digital properties. We do not collect, retain, or process special categories of personal data unless explicitly required by law.

All website traffic is routed through industry-standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption. Personal data is encrypted both at rest and in transit using contemporary cryptographic protocols. All payment transactions are processed through PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment service providers; the Company does not store, process, or transmit cardholder data on its own servers.

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC does not sell, rent, or share corporate partner data, customer data, or any personally identifiable information with unaffiliated third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. Data is shared only with authorized service providers operating under binding data processing agreements, and solely to fulfill the operational and legal obligations of the Company.

Data subjects retain the right to access, correct, port, restrict, or request deletion of their personal information at any time, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. Verified requests may be directed to hello@oaahfashiongroup.com and will be processed within the statutory response windows mandated by the applicable jurisdiction.

03

Supply Chain Transparency

+

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC is committed to ethical labor practices and full vendor compliance across every tier of our global supply chain. The Company maintains a formal zero-tolerance policy with respect to forced labor, indentured labor, child labor, human trafficking, and any other practice that violates internationally recognized human rights standards or applicable labor laws.

All manufacturing partners, logistics providers, and third-party vendors engaged by the Company are required to operate in strict accordance with the OAAHFASHIONGROUP Supplier Code of Conduct. This includes, without limitation, adherence to fair wage requirements, lawful working hours, safe and humane working conditions, freedom of association, and the absolute prohibition of any form of involuntary labor.

The Company conducts strict vendor compliance screening prior to onboarding any new supplier and performs annual operational audits on its active vendor network. Audit findings are reviewed by management; material non-compliance results in immediate corrective action plans and, where warranted, the termination of the vendor relationship.

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC further commits to ongoing diligence regarding the provenance of raw materials, the legitimacy of manufacturing facilities, and the integrity of intermediary logistics partners, in alignment with internationally recognized supply chain transparency frameworks.

04

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) & KYC Policy

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC affirms its full and ongoing commitment to combating money laundering, terrorist financing, sanctions evasion, and financial fraud. OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC does not knowingly conduct business with any sanctioned individual, entity, or jurisdiction, and engages exclusively with regulated financial counterparties for the processing of corporate and commercial transactions.
