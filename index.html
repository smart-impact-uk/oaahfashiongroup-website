Governance, Compliance & Disclosures

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC operates in full accordance with applicable corporate, consumer protection, and data privacy regulations. The disclosures below summarize our standing policies.

01

Privacy Policy

+

OAAHFASHIONGROUP LLC is committed to safeguarding the personal information of its customers, partners, and stakeholders. Our data practices are aligned with global data protection regulations, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

We adhere to the principle of data minimization, collecting only information strictly necessary for transactional processing, regulatory compliance, and operational continuity. All payment transactions are routed through PCI-DSS compliant payment processors; we do not store cardholder data on our servers. Personal data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard cryptographic protocols.
